Privacy policy

Data controller

Roman Adhumeau, Le Mans Conciergerie, lemansconciergerie@gmail.com.

Data collected

Through the contact form: name, phone, email, area and type of property, message, and the page the request came from. The pricing calculator collects nothing: everything is calculated in your browser.

Why

Only to contact you about your property and arrange a visit. Legal basis: your consent, given by ticking the box on the form.

Who receives it

Le Mans Conciergerie only. Sending goes through the Web3Forms service, which forwards the message to our mailbox. No data is sold, rented or passed on.

Retention period

3 years after our last contact if you don't become a client. If you become a client: the length of the contract, then the periods required by law (invoices: 10 years).

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction and portability: write to lemansconciergerie@gmail.com. You can also contact the French data protection authority, CNIL (cnil.fr).

Cookies

This site sets no audience-measurement or advertising cookies, so there is no cookie banner. Your language choice may be remembered in your browser, only for convenience. If measurement tools are added one day, your consent will be asked first.
