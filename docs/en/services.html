What I do between every stay

  • Full cleaning

    After every check-out, with a room-by-room checklist.

  • Hotel linen

    Sheets and towels supplied, washed and changed for every stay.

  • Welcome box

    And supplies restocked: paper, soap, coffee.

    Sent to your phone after every cleaning.
