FAQ

Owners’ questions about our concierge service in Le Mans

Trust, weekends, pricing, commitment, guests’ money: twelve straight answers. Another question? Write to me.

Read the answersAsk my question

The answers

You're a new business: why should I trust you?

We meet at your property before you sign anything, the first cleaning is free, you get photos after every cleaning, and I hold professional liability insurance (certificate on request).

What happens if nobody turns up on a Saturday evening?

Weekends are covered by independent partner cleaners. If the property isn't ready by check-in time, the cleaning for that stay is free.

Why don't you handle my messages and prices?

Because you stay in charge. You remain the only one in control of your listing and your money: that's the difference with a traditional concierge service.

How much will I pay?

15% of your stays, plus the cleaning and linen fee per stay (paid by your guests if you add it as a cleaning fee). Try the calculator.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No. You can stop with 30 days' notice.

How do you know when my guests arrive?

You share your listing calendar once (iCal link, read-only). I see check-in and check-out dates; I have no access to your payments.

Who collects the guests' money?

You do, directly. I never receive money on your behalf. I send you one invoice a month.

Where do you work?

In Le Mans and the surrounding towns: Allonnes, Coulaines, Arnage, Changé, Sargé-lès-Le Mans, La Chapelle-Saint-Aubin, Ruaudin, Yvré-l'Évêque, Saint-Saturnin, Mulsanne. Another town nearby? Just ask.

Are linen and supplies included?

Yes, in the cleaning + linen fee: hotel-quality sheets and towels, washed and changed for every stay. The welcome box items are included too. Equipment you buy for the property is paid by you.

I only want cleaning: is that possible?

Yes, with the cleaning-only option: you pay only the cleaning fee per stay, with no commission, and you handle everything else.

What about the 24 Hours of Le Mans?

It's the busiest week of the year. Book your cleaning as soon as your guests have booked: places are limited in June.

What if something breaks or gets damaged?

I let you know with photos. For a small repair I can do it myself (€30/hour); otherwise I suggest a tradesperson. Nothing is done without your approval.

Let's meet at your property

One visit, no commitment. The first cleaning is free.
