Commission

In bands of the month's booking total, like income tax: 10% from €0 to €800, 15% from €800 to €1,500, 20% from €1,500 to €2,500, 25% above that. Each rate only applies to its own band. Example: for €2,000 of bookings, €80 + €105 + €100 = €285 excl. VAT.

Photos and an optimised listing on Airbnb and Booking.com, replies to guests, check-ins and key handover, cleaning before every stay, a walk-through after every check-out and handling of problems: if there is damage, we call in a specialist and send you a quote. Our advice on your property is also included.

Yes: €150 excl. VAT a month as soon as there is at least one stay. For example, €1,000 of bookings gives €80 + €30 = €110, so the €150 minimum applies. A month with no stays costs you nothing.
